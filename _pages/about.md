Zhan Chen (Member, IEEE) received his Ph.D. degree from the Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an, China, in 2025. He is now a postdoctoral researcher at the College of Intelligence 
Science and Technology in National University of Defense Technology.

Research Direction
======
1. Multi-agent consensus theory
2. Information fusion and state estimation
3. Navigation, guidance and control of aircraft
